Rendering
---------
Drawn with Unicode block-element glyphs. Each glyph is one terminal cell; a
good terminal tiles them seamlessly (the mockups render them as filled
rectangles to prove the shape). Half-cell detail is available via block glyphs
(▀ ▄ ▌ ▐ ▖ ▗ ▘ ▝ ▙ ▟ ▛ ▜), quarter detail via the quadrant glyphs.
